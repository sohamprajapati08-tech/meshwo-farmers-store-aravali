Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Store & RBI Guidelines

Welcome to MESHWO FARMERS (Operated by MESHWO FARMER PRODUCER CO. LTD). We are an indigenous tribal women farmer collective based in Shamlaji, Aravalli district, Gujarat, dedicated to delivering 100% pure, sacred Vedic forest harvest directly to families across India.

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our website, mobile application, or online checkout services.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or contact our farm coordinators, we collect the following necessary information:

  • Personal Contact Information: Full name, mobile/WhatsApp phone number, and optional email address for digital delivery receipts.
  • Delivery Address: Street address, landmark, city, state, and pincode required for courier and postal dispatch of fresh organic harvest.
  • Transaction Data: Payment method selected (Cash on Delivery, UPI Reference Number, or Online Hosted Gateway Payment ID) and total payable amount.
  • Device & App Information: Minimal technical information required for app functionality and progressive web app (PWA) cache operations.

2. Payment & Financial Data Security

We strictly uphold the highest standards of financial security:

  • Zero Sensitive Storage: We never store your Credit/Debit Card numbers, CVV codes, or NetBanking passwords on our servers.
  • 256-Bit SSL Encrypted Gateways: All online payment sessions are processed through certified, RBI-licensed payment gateways (such as Razorpay and Stripe) via automated 256-bit SSL encrypted hosted checkout tunnels.
  • Direct UPI Safety: For UPI payments, transactions occur directly within your trusted UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use customer data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To prepare, package, and dispatch your sacred forest produce orders from our Shamlaji farm center.
  • To send order confirmation and real-time tracking updates via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To provide dedicated customer support regarding farm harvest purity, batch lab tests, and deliveries.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders or unauthorized transactions.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties or advertisers.

Information is shared strictly with trusted logistics partners (e.g., India Post / Speed Post / Courier Partners) solely for doorstep parcel delivery, and with our payment gateway providers to verify authentic payments.

5. Google Play Store & App Permissions

Our mobile application complies fully with the Google Play Store Developer Program Policies:

  • The app does not request invasive permissions (such as contacts, microphone, camera, or background location).
  • Internet access is used exclusively to fetch farm products, process orders, and display verified NABL purity reports.

6. Data Retention & Customer Rights

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your account and personal details from our active database at any time. To request account deletion or data removal, please contact our support team.
